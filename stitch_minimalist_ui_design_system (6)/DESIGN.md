## Brand & Style

This design system defines an understated, physical, and tranquil digital travel companion. It captures the tactile sensation of smooth debossed cardstock, high-grade matte travel instruments, and calm daylight over coastal horizons.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Tactile & Physical:** Grounded in a modern, restrained interpretation of soft neumorphism. Components do not mimic glossy plastic; rather, they feel sculpted from matte, chalk-warm stone and heavyweight uncoated paper.
- **Calm & Discerning:** Free of hyperactive gamification, artificial vibrancy, and aggressive gradients. It induces stillness and confidence during transit.
- **Precision Travel Tool:** Highly functional, legibly organized, and devoid of synthetic decorative flourishes.

### Aesthetic Movement
The aesthetic synthesizes **Soft Tactile Neumorphism** with **Clean Editorial Minimalism**. Dual-directional light signatures (subtle warm highlights paired with soft directional drop-shadows) create authentic depth, while generous whitespace and structured typography maintain sharp legibility.

## Colors

The palette discards generic saturated app tones, teals, and terracotta oranges in favor of an airy, coastal-altitude sky spectrum anchored by an organic, warm limestone substrate.

### Primary Palette & Foundation
- **Base Canvas (`#fbf9f5`):** Warm, chalky parchment neutral. Serves as the global backdrop to prevent eye strain under outdoor daylight.
- **Surface Sculpted (`#f4f0e6`):** Slightly deeper limestone neutral. Used for elevated cards, extruded panels, and interactive tactile containers.
- **Primary Sky Accent (`#0284c7`):** Deep cerulean. Used for active wayfinding indicators, focal action buttons, and critical states.
- **Secondary Atmospheric (`#0369a1`):** Deep oceanic cerulean for authoritative text headers, active state boundaries, and high-contrast badges.
- **Tertiary Horizon (`#38bdf8`):** Crisp sky blue highlight for interactive focus rings, active indicator pips, and subtle rim lights.
- **Subtle Fill (`#e0f2fe`):** Frosted sky tint for depressed chip states, tag backdrops, and active schedule blocks.

### Neutral Shades & Text Contrast
- **Text Dominant (`#1c1917`):** Warm graphite for optimal readability on warm paper backgrounds.
- **Text Muted (`#78716c`):** Soft stone gray for flight metadata, timestamps, and secondary coordinates.
- **Highlight Edge (`#ffffff`):** Pure white light specular for the top-left directional bevels.
- **Shadow Edge (`#e2ddd2`):** Warm earthen shadow tint for the bottom-right directional depth.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** is the sole typographic voice across all hierarchy levels. Its geometry provides modern clarity, while its soft terminal curves echo the sculpted radii of the physical component architecture.

### Typographic Principles
- **Airport Code & Data Rigor:** Airport codes (e.g., `HND`, `SFO`, `CPH`), gate numbers, and platform trackers utilize `label-sm` or `headline-md` set in all-caps with generous letter spacing (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) to ensure instant glanceability in motion.
- **Rhythmic Vertical Cadence:** All line-heights lock cleanly to an 8px and 4px baseline grid.
- **Restrained Weights:** Headlines never exceed weight 700. Structural stability is maintained through weight 600, preserving the calm, non-hyperbolic tone.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8-point structural system, maintaining generous negative space to allow tactile surfaces room to "cast" light and shadow without visual collision.

### Grid Architecture
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Stacked single-column card flows emphasize thumb-driven travel interactions.
- **Tablet (768px–1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Accommodates split views (e.g., itinerary timeline on the left, map details on the right).
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column layout capped at `76rem` (`1216px`), centered with dynamic external padding. Provides a spacious 3-tier layout: primary navigation dock, active itinerary stage, and deep travel insight panel.

### Neumorphic Spatial Cushion
Because embossed and debossed surfaces rely on subtle dual-gradient shadows, components must maintain at least `0.75rem` (`space-sm`) of breathing room between bounding boxes to prevent overlapping shadow artifacts.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through controlled light angles rather than flat color layering or heavy black drop-shadows. The light source is strictly fixed at the top-left (135-degree simulated solar vector).

### Dual Light Engine
Every raised surface pairs a translucent white specular highlight on its top-left perimeter with an earthen warm tone on its bottom-right base.

1. **Extruded Level 1 (Resting Cards, Tiles, Panels):**
   - Background: `#f4f0e6`
   - Shadows:
     - Top-Left: `-6px -6px 14px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`
     - Bottom-Right: `6px 6px 16px rgba(214, 206, 190, 0.65)`

2. **Extruded Level 2 (Floating Action Trays, Active Modals, Itinerary Drawers):**
   - Background: `#f4f0e6`
   - Shadows:
     - Top-Left: `-10px -10px 24px rgba(255, 255, 255, 1)`
     - Bottom-Right: `12px 12px 28px rgba(200, 190, 172, 0.55)`

3. **Debossed / Inset Level (Input Wells, Search Bars, Toggled-down Chips):**
   - Background: `#eeeae0`
   - Inner Shadows:
     - Top-Left: `inset 4px 4px 8px rgba(204, 195, 178, 0.6)`
     - Bottom-Right: `inset -4px -4px 8px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`

4. **Accented Elevation (Primary Action Anchors):**
   - Background: `#0284c7`
   - Shadows:
     - Top-Left: `-4px -4px 10px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`
     - Bottom-Right: `6px 6px 14px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.35)`

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded** shape geometry (`roundedness: 2`). Radii match the curvature of smooth river stones and premium travel hardware.

### Radius System
- **Base Components (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Checkboxes, status badges, small utility chips, and notification pips.
- **Medium Elements (`1rem` / `16px`):** Primary and secondary action buttons, input fields, flight segment blocks, and ticket notch dividers.
- **Large Panels (`1.5rem` / `24px`):** Boarding pass cards, itinerary schedule boards, interactive map sheets, and navigation shells.
- **Pill Formats (`9999px`):** Categorical search filter toggles, transit state indicators, and floating back-to-top anchors.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#0284c7` cerulean body with white text. Elevated with subtle directional ambient glow (`6px 6px 14px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.35)` and `-3px -3px 8px #ffffff`). On active press, transitions to an inset inner shadow (`inset 2px 2px 5px rgba(3, 105, 161, 0.5)`), visually depressing into the interface.
- **Secondary Tactile Button:** Sculpted `#f4f0e6` surface with `#0369a1` text. Employs Extruded Level 1 dual shadows. Depresses cleanly into an inset surface on click or tap.

### Chips & Filter Toggles
- **Default Inactive:** Rounded pill in `#f4f0e6` with soft extrusion. Text is stone neutral (`#78716c`).
- **Selected State:** Shifts immediately to a debossed state with inner shadow, filled with `#e0f2fe`. Text shifts to crisp cerulean `#0369a1`, with a `4px` indicator dot in `#38bdf8`.

### Flight & Transit Cards
- Formed from `#f4f0e6` base with 24px corner radii. 
- Embossed softly against the `#fbf9f5` page canvas.
- Features a subtle micro-debossed perforation line separating the departure/arrival details from the boarding barcode or seat assignment.
- Accent pips and flight route progression bars use `#0284c7` on tracks debossed into the card surface.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Inverted physical well. Implements Inset Level depth with background `#eeeae0`.
- Text color is `#1c1917` with `#78716c` placeholder text.
- Focus state activates an outer perimeter ring in sky blue (`#38bdf8` at 2px width) without breaking the interior debossed light depth.

### Lists & Timelines
- Waypoint items rest on vertical continuous channels debossed into the background canvas.
- Milestone nodes are raised circular discs (`16px`) with center pips of `#0284c7`.
- Hovering or selecting a list row raises it from flat alignment to an Extruded Level 1 card via smooth 200ms ease transitions.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Radio Buttons:** Circular inset wells (`20px`). When checked, a raised `#0284c7` sphere with a crisp white specular dot snaps into the center.
- **Checkboxes:** Square inset wells (`20px`, `6px` radius). When active, fills with `#0284c7` featuring a clean white checkmark icon.

### Travel Companion Specific: Itinerary Segment Drawer
- An anchored, bottom-sheet sliding tray built with Extruded Level 2 depth.
- Incorporates a tactile debossed top-handle pill (`36px x 4px`) for gesture-driven collapse and expansion.
- Flight delay or on-time status tags use deep cerulean typography over `#e0f2fe` debossed badges, strictly avoiding green and red urgency cues in favor of calm, legible states.
